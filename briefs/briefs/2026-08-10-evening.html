Hurlston's key takeaways on FCC supply chain regulation: 1. Multiple formal dialogues between Lumentum leadership and FCC senior staff show commission staff recognize the severe AI infrastructure supply shock a broad optical ban would create. 2. Full rulemaking procedural barriers: The NPRM requires multimonth public comment periods, 5 FCC commissioner unanimous voting, cross-agency coordination with Commerce Department export control teams. There is insufficient legislative timeline to enact sweeping optical hardware restrictions before the end of the current administration's term. 4.
